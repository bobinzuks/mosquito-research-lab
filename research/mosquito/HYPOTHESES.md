# Competing hypotheses — NOT established conclusions

## H-WIND-1: Odor plume disruption
Wind changes host-attractant concentration and distribution, reducing successful orientation. Primary supporting source: DOI 10.1603/0022-2585-40.5.607. Alternative: direct flight limitation. Predictions must distinguish odor dilution from mechanical effects.

## H-HUM-1: Humidity/temperature integration
Short-range humidity and heat sensing contribute to host proximity detection. Primary source: DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2022.12.025. Do not infer that electronic stimulation can substitute for humidity.

## H-EFIELD-1: Mechanical vs sensory effects
An electric-field barrier may alter mosquito movement through physical forces, sensory detection, or both. Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11424001/. The mechanism remains unresolved in this lab. No autonomous physical experiments.

## H-RAIN-1: Rain survival vs anticipation
Surviving raindrop collisions is distinct from predicting rainfall or responding to rain sounds. Primary source: DOI 10.1073/pnas.1205446109.

## H-SOUND-1: Hearing is context-dependent
Male responses to mating flight tones do not establish female host-seeking repellency. Primary source: DOI 10.1098/rsif.2022.0285.

## Research integrity
Evidence supporting one hypothesis must not be used to assert a different mechanism. Require species and assay compatibility, alternative explanations, and negative results.
