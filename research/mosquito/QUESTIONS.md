# Initial research questions

These are hypotheses and investigation topics, **not verified claims**.

1. What sensory mechanisms allow mosquitoes to detect airflow and mechanical disturbances?
2. Which experimental observations establish responses to rain, humidity and pressure changes?
3. Are electrostatic-field effects explained by physical forces, sensory detection, or both?
4. What mosquito-specific evidence exists for responses to audio, magnetic, RF or terahertz signals?
5. Which sensory inputs converge during host-seeking and landing decisions?
6. What findings are species-specific and which replicate across independent laboratories?

## Evidence protocol
For each paper, record DOI/URL, species, experimental conditions, measurement, controls, replication, limitations, and whether the result is independently confirmed.
